## Name

## Subtitle

## Promotional text

A more legible menu bar battery for macOS 27, with modern and classic designs, flexible percentage placement, cycle count, and charging alerts.

## Description

Better Battery makes your Mac’s battery status easier to read at a glance.

macOS 27 introduces a clean new battery design, but placing the percentage inside the system icon can make the number harder to see. Better Battery keeps the modern look while showing the percentage clearly beside the battery.

Make the menu bar yours:

• Choose the modern macOS 27 battery or a classic outlined design

• Place the percentage on the left or right

• Hide the percent symbol

• Show only the percentage alongside Apple’s battery indicator

• See an accurate fill level and charging indicator

Get useful alerts in the direction that matters. Better Battery can notify you when the battery drops to a selected level while discharging, or reaches a selected level while charging. Start with the included 20% low-battery and 80% charging defaults, choose from common presets, or set your own thresholds.

Better Battery also shows your battery cycle count, and it can open automatically when you log in.

Designed specifically for macOS 27 and Apple silicon.

## App Review notes

Better Battery is a menu bar utility and intentionally does not display a Dock icon. After launch, its battery indicator appears in the menu bar. Click the indicator and choose Settings to access General, Notifications, and Battery Health.

The app reads battery and power-source information locally using macOS system APIs. Preferences remain on the Mac, no account or login is required, and the app does not collect user data.

Notification permission is requested when the user enables battery notifications. The default thresholds are 20% while discharging and 80% while charging. The two directions are independent.

To test charging behavior, connect or disconnect the Mac from power. Battery Health displays the cycle count reported by macOS. Other health fields appear only when macOS exposes them through its public battery interface.

This Mac App Store build does not include Sparkle or external update functionality. Updates are delivered exclusively through the Mac App Store.
